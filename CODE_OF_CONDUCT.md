# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

RiskSense exists to help people understand danger and reach help. Everyone who
contributes — maintainers, translators, data-source authors, reporters of bugs —
deserves a harassment-free experience regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this community:

- Being accurate and honest, especially about uncertainty and about mistakes.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assuming good faith and asking questions before concluding someone is wrong.
- Respecting that this is public-safety software: people may rely on it.
- Prioritising the safety and clarity of the end user over being right in an
  argument.

Behaviour that is unacceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Deliberately introducing false, misleading or unverifiable hazard information.
- Pressuring maintainers to overstate confidence, coverage or capability.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Submitting fabricated emergency contact data or fabricated event data.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They
will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behaviour they
deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful, including removing
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and also when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers
through the repository's private reporting channel, or by opening a confidential
issue if a private channel is unavailable. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the
privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction.** A private warning explaining the violation, and a request for
   a public apology if the behaviour was public.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including a
   period of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban from any public interaction within the
   project.
